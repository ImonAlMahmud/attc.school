Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how ATTC handles your information.

Effective Date: 5th July, 2025

We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all our students, applicants, and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary for registration, course administration, and communication purposes. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Age and date of birth
  • Contact details (phone number, address, email)
  • Educational background
  • National identification (e.g., NID or passport)
  • Emergency contact information

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used only for:

  • Processing course registrations and enrollments
  • Providing training and support services
  • Internal record-keeping and certification purposes
  • Contacting students regarding course updates, schedules, or important notices

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

ATTC does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your information is kept confidential and used solely for the purposes mentioned above. We may, however, be required to disclose information if:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of ATTC, its students, or the public

4. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to ensure the security of your personal information. This includes:

  • Restricted access to student data
  • Secured storage of physical documents
  • Use of password-protected systems for digital records

However, while we strive to protect your information, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Use of Photos and Media

During training and events, photos or videos may be taken for promotional or documentation purposes. If you do not wish to appear in such materials, please inform our administration team in writing.

6. Access and Updates to Your Information

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Ask for corrections if the data we hold is inaccurate or outdated
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal and administrative requirements)

To do so, please contact us at the details provided below.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

Our services may include links to third-party websites (e.g., job portals, external certifiers). This Privacy Policy does not cover how those third parties collect or use your data. We encourage you to read their privacy policies separately.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be communicated through our official notice boards, website, or email.
