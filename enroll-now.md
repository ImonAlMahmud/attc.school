# Enroll Now — Vocational Course Admission | ATTC Dhaka

Apply for ISO-certified trade training programs at Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC).

---

## Available Trade Courses for Enrollment

1. **Plasterer**: Comprehensive plastering and finishing skills.
2. **Tiler**: Floor, wall, and mosaic tile installation.
3. **Formwork Carpentry**: Concrete shuttering and structural carpentry.
4. **Bar Bending**: Rebar cutting, bending schedules, and reinforcement fixing.
5. **Bricklayer**: Masonry bonds, mortar techniques, and structural brickwork.

---

## Admission Requirements

- **Age**: Minimum 18 years old.
- **Physical Fitness**: Ability to perform hands-on construction trade activities.
- **Identification**: National ID (NID) or Passport / Birth Certificate copy.
- **Photographs**: Recent passport-size photographs.

## Benefits of Enrolling at ATTC

- **ISO Certified Curriculum**: Real-world practical training meeting local and overseas industry benchmarks.
- **Hostel & Residential Amenities**: Safe on-campus boarding for up to 200 trainees.
- **Career Support**: Skill certification and international employment placement facilitation.
- **Experienced Trainers**: Instruction by certified master craftsmen and industry supervisors.
