# Vocational Training Courses — ATTC Dhaka

Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC) offers 5 specialized, ISO-certified vocational construction trade courses designed to prepare trainees for prestigious careers in Bangladesh and abroad.

---

## Course Catalog

### 1. [Plasterer Course](https://www.attc.school/courses/plasterer.html)
- **Trade Area**: Construction Finishing & Surface Preparation
- **Summary**: Comprehensive practical training in cement, lime, and gypsum plastering, surface leveling, decorative finishes, and skim coating.
- **Key Modules**: Surface preparation, mixing ratios, application techniques, quality finishing, job safety.
- **Certification**: ATTC Certified Plasterer
- **Full Details**: [plasterer.html](https://www.attc.school/courses/plasterer.html)

### 2. [Tiler Course](https://www.attc.school/courses/tiler.html)
- **Trade Area**: Floor & Wall Tiling
- **Summary**: Professional tile laying for floors, walls, bathrooms, kitchens, and decorative mosaic patterns.
- **Key Modules**: Subfloor preparation, adhesive application, precision tile cutting, laser alignment, grouting & sealing.
- **Certification**: ATTC Certified Tiler
- **Full Details**: [tiler.html](https://www.attc.school/courses/tiler.html)

### 3. [Formwork Carpentry Course](https://www.attc.school/courses/formwork-carpentry.html)
- **Trade Area**: Structural Concrete Formwork & Shuttering
- **Summary**: Fabrication, assembly, erection, and dismantling of timber, plywood, and steel formwork systems for multi-story concrete structures.
- **Key Modules**: Blueprint reading, shuttering fabrication, column and beam formwork, shoring systems, safe stripping.
- **Certification**: ATTC Certified Formwork Carpenter
- **Full Details**: [formwork-carpentry.html](https://www.attc.school/courses/formwork-carpentry.html)

### 4. [Bar Bending Course](https://www.attc.school/courses/bar-bending.html)
- **Trade Area**: Reinforced Steel Rebar & Structural Fixing
- **Summary**: Steel rebar cutting, manual and motorized bending, bar marking, bending schedules, and reinforcement cage assembly for foundation footings, columns, and slabs.
- **Key Modules**: Rebar grades & diameters, blueprint reading, mechanical bending jigs, tying schedules, site safety.
- **Certification**: ATTC Certified Bar Bender
- **Full Details**: [bar-bending.html](https://www.attc.school/courses/bar-bending.html)

### 5. [Bricklayer Course](https://www.attc.school/courses/bricklayer.html)
- **Trade Area**: Masonry & Structural Brickwork
- **Summary**: Professional bricklaying techniques, English & Flemish bonds, mortar preparation, arch construction, joint finishing, and plumb alignment.
- **Key Modules**: Masonry materials & mortars, bond patterns, corner building, damp-proof coursing, structural walling.
- **Certification**: ATTC Certified Bricklayer
- **Full Details**: [bricklayer.html](https://www.attc.school/courses/bricklayer.html)
