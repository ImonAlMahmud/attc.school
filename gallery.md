# Campus Gallery & Facilities — ATTC Dhaka

Explore the modern campus, dynamic workshops, student accommodations, and training environments at Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC).

---

## Campus Infrastructure

- **Outdoor Campus View**: 6-Bigha modern training and testing grounds in Bashundhara C/A, Madani Avenue, Dhaka.
- **Trainee Dormitory**: Modern residential buildings accommodating 200+ students with individual storage and clean amenities.
- **Spacious Practical Workshop Bays**: Large ventilated trade workshop sheds equipped with industrial-grade tools.
- **Modern Classrooms**: Multi-media lecture halls for theoretical lessons and technical drawing analysis.
- **Clean Dining & Canteen**: Dedicated cafeteria preparing fresh daily meals.
- **Medical & First Aid Facility**: On-site medical support room for prompt health care.

---

## Trade Practical Training in Action

- **Plastering Workshops**: Trainees mastering wall leveling, cement plastering, and float finishes.
- **Tiling Practice Areas**: Floor and bathroom mockup bays for precision tile laying.
- **Formwork Assembly Yards**: Shuttering and scaffolding erection practice for multi-story mockups.
- **Rebar Bending & Fabrication**: Motorized rebar cutters and manual bending machines in operation.
- **Bricklaying Bays**: Brick bonding, plumb alignment, and corner erection workshops.
