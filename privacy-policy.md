# Privacy Policy — Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC)

**Effective Date**: July 5, 2025  
**Domain**: https://www.attc.school

ATTC is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all our students, applicants, trainees, and website visitors. This policy explains how we collect, handle, and safeguard your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary for course admissions, student verification, training administration, and communications:
- Full Name, Age, and Date of Birth
- Contact details (Phone number, physical address, email)
- Educational background and prior trade experience
- National ID (NID) or Passport / Birth Certificate data
- Emergency contact information and medical notes (for workshop safety)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Processing course registrations, identity verification, and enrollments
- Administering vocational training workshops, evaluations, and trade assessments
- Issuing official certificates of completion and transcripts
- Assisting students with authorized job placement programs
- Communicating schedule updates, notices, and institutional announcements

---

## 3. Data Protection & Sharing

- **Zero Selling/Renting**: ATTC does not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties.
- **Confidentiality**: All records are maintained confidentially in accordance with applicable Bangladesh data privacy standards.
- **Legal Compliance**: Data is disclosed only when mandated by applicable law or regulatory authority.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement strict administrative, physical, and digital security measures:
- Role-based access control for administrative staff
- Secure digital storage and encrypted transmissions
- Secured storage for physical admissions documents
