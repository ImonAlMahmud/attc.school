Course Overview

Specialize in constructing, installing, and dismantling concrete formwork systems. An indispensable trade in large-scale construction — walls, slabs, beams, columns, and complex structures.

Course Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Understand structural blueprints and formwork specifications
  • Build and assemble timber and metal formwork for reinforced concrete
  • Ensure alignment, bracing, and safety under heavy concrete loads
  • Master safe dismantling and material reuse procedures

Course Curriculum

  • Role of formwork in structural concrete work
  • Tools, timber, and metal formwork systems
  • Understanding structural blueprints and specifications
  • Measuring and laying out formwork positions
  • Building forms for slabs, beams, columns, and walls
  • Bracing, shoring, and tie rod installations
  • Erecting shuttering systems on site
  • Ensuring moisture-tight and pressure-resistant joints
  • Stripping forms safely post-curing
  • PPE and site safety regulations

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the Formwork Carpentry course can pursue a variety of career paths, including:

  • Professional Formwork Carpenter
  • Structural Shuttering Specialist
  • Site Foreman / Team Lead
  • Commercial Building Contractor
