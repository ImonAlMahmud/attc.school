## Course Overview

The Bricklayer course at ATTC is crafted to turn aspiring learners into skilled masonry craftsmen. Bricklaying is a foundational pillar of civil construction, essential for erecting durable, weather-resistant, and aesthetically pleasing structures. This intensive course teaches brick bonds, mortar preparation, joint alignment, and plumb accuracy under the mentorship of seasoned instructors.

---

## Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Understand different types of bricks, blocks, and mortar mixtures
- Master standard brickwork bonds (English bond, Flemish bond, Stretcher bond)
- Build straight walls, corners, piers, and decorative brick arches
- Read architectural drawings and compute material estimates
- Ensure structural verticality, horizontal level, and gauge consistency
- Work safely on scaffolding following rigorous site safety protocols

---

## Course Curriculum & Modules

1. **Introduction to Masonry**
   - Fundamentals of masonry and history of brickwork
   - Types of building bricks, clay blocks, and concrete blocks
   - Tool identification: trowels, spirit levels, plumb bobs, line and pins

2. **Materials & Mortar Preparation**
   - Cement, sand, and lime ratios for various applications
   - Water-cement ratio and slump testing
   - Mortar mixing by hand and mechanical batch mixers

3. **Basic Bricklaying Techniques**
   - Setting out foundations and corner leads (quoins)
   - Laying stretcher and header courses
   - Joint finishing (flush, bucket handle, weather struck, recessed)

4. **Advanced Masonry Skills**
   - English and Flemish bonding patterns
   - Cavity wall construction with insulation and ties
   - Constructing semicircular and segmental arches
   - Damp-proof course (DPC) installation

5. **Blueprint Reading & Site Geometry**
   - Reading building elevations and plan drawings
   - Setting right angles (3:4:5 method) and elevation datums
   - Calculating brick and mortar quantities

6. **Safety & On-Site Practice**
   - Working at heights and scaffolding safety
   - Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
   - Waste minimization and site cleanliness
