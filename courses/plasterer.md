## Course Overview

The Plasterer course at ATTC equips students with advanced skills in applying internal and external plaster finishes on masonry and concrete structures. Plastering provides smooth, durable, and protective finishes for walls and ceilings, essential for both aesthetic appeal and environmental protection.

---

## Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Prepare diverse wall and ceiling substrates for plaster application
- Mix cement, lime, gypsum, and pre-mixed plaster formulas to exact consistencies
- Apply scratch coats, brown coats, and fine finishing coats with precision
- Level surfaces using screeds, straight edges, and feather edges
- Execute decorative plaster moldings, cornices, and exterior stucco finishes
- Repair cracked, damaged, or moisture-affected plaster surfaces

---

## Course Curriculum & Modules

1. **Introduction & Tools of the Trade**
   - Plastering principles and substrate compatibility
   - Hand tools: hawks, trowels, floats (wooden, sponge, plastic), corner trowels
   - Surface prep tools: wire brushes, scarifiers, bonding agents

2. **Plaster Materials & Mortar Formulations**
   - Cement-sand mortar ratios for interior vs. exterior walls
   - Gypsum plaster (POP), lime wash, and ready-mix skim coats
   - Retarders, plasticizers, and waterproofing admixtures

3. **Surface Preparation & Screed Alignment**
   - Raking out mortar joints and hacking concrete surfaces for keying
   - Applying bonding agents (SBR / Acrylic primers)
   - Establishing vertical guide screeds and level bull points

4. **Multi-Coat Plastering Techniques**
   - Applying the initial scratch coat and combing for mechanical bond
   - Applying and leveling the floating (intermediate) coat
   - Applying the final skim coat to a mirror-smooth finish

5. **External Rendering & Decorative Work**
   - Sand-faced and roughcast exterior finishes (stucco)
   - Corner beading and expansion joint installation
   - Architectural cornices, grooves, and drip bands

6. **Safety, Quality Assurance & Cleanliness**
   - Scaffolding safety and dust protection
   - Curing methods for optimal plaster strength
   - Defect inspection (crazing, efflorescence, hollow spots)
