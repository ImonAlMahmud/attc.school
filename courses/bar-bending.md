## Course Overview

The Bar Bending course at ATTC is designed to provide you with the essential skills and knowledge to become a proficient bar bender in the construction industry. Bar bending is a fundamental skill for constructing reinforced concrete structures, which are vital for building safe, durable, and stable buildings. This hands-on course focuses on mastering the techniques of bending, cutting, and shaping steel bars (rebar) to precise specifications for use in foundations, beams, columns, and other reinforced concrete elements.

---

## Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Understand the role of bar bending in reinforced concrete construction
- Interpret and follow structural drawings related to rebar and reinforcement
- Accurately measure, cut, and bend steel bars according to specifications
- Use bar bending machines and manual tools safely and efficiently
- Ensure proper reinforcement of concrete elements such as slabs, beams, and columns
- Maintain safety standards and follow best practices while working on-site
- Be prepared to work on large-scale construction projects both locally and internationally

---

## Course Curriculum & Modules

1. **Introduction to Bar Bending**
   - Importance of reinforcement in concrete construction
   - Reinforcing bar types (Mild steel, TMT bars, high-yield rebar)
   - Overview of tools and workshop machinery
   - Health and safety regulations

2. **Rebar Types and Specifications**
   - Understanding bar diameter, length, and tensile strength
   - Reading structural construction drawings
   - Bar marking systems and bending schedules

3. **Bar Bending Techniques**
   - Manual and machine-based bending methods
   - Bending bars to precise angles and shapes (90°, 135°, 180°)
   - Bending jigs and motorized bending machines

4. **Rebar Cutting and Handling**
   - Cutting techniques for various diameters
   - Safe operation of shears, motorized cutters, and saws
   - Safe handling, stacking, and storage of steel

5. **Bar Tying and Reinforcement Layout**
   - Wire tying techniques and binding patterns
   - Constructing reinforcement cages for columns, beams, and footings
   - Ensuring proper spacing, cover blocks, and overlap splice lengths

6. **Site Safety & Quality Control**
   - Site safety protocols and mandatory PPE
   - Quality checks and dimensional tolerance verification
   - Team coordination on active construction sites
