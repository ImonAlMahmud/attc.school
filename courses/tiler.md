## Course Overview

The Tiler course at ATTC delivers comprehensive practical training in installing ceramic, porcelain, mosaic, granite, and marble tiles on walls, floors, kitchens, bathrooms, and exterior façades. Precision layout, cutting techniques, substrate preparation, and waterproofing are taught through realistic workshop simulations.

---

## Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Inspect, level, and prepare concrete floors and plaster walls for tile adhesion
- Set out symmetrical tile layouts using centerline methods and laser levels
- Cut and shape tiles accurately using manual score-and-snap cutters, wet tile saws, and angle grinders
- Mix and apply specialized tile adhesives, thin-set mortars, and waterproof membranes
- Install floor and wall tiles with uniform grout lines, lippage prevention, and perfect slope to drains
- Grout, seal, and clean tiled surfaces to international architectural standards

## Course Curriculum & Modules

1. **Tile Materials & Substrate Science**
   - Tile types: Ceramic, vitrified, porcelain, mosaic, quarry, marble, and granite
   - Adhesives: Cementitious adhesives (C1, C2), epoxy adhesives, latex additives
   - Substrate testing: moisture check, level tolerances, deflection

2. **Tools of the Tiling Craft**
   - Notched trowels (square, U-notch, V-notch) and proper adhesive coverage
   - Manual tile cutters, diamond blade wet saws, nippers, and hole saws
   - Tile leveling clips, wedges, spacers, and suction cups

3. **Setting Out & Layout Geometry**
   - Finding room centerlines and avoiding awkward narrow cuts
   - Setting datum lines and laser leveling for wall tiles
   - Slope and gradient setting for shower trays and wet areas

4. **Tile Installation Techniques**
   - Floor tiling methods (buttering back, comb spreading)
   - Wall tiling methods and batten installation
   - Mitering edges (45° cuts) for clean external tile corners

5. **Waterproofing & Wet Area Management**
   - Liquid-applied waterproofing membranes and corner tape
   - Sealing pipe penetrations and drain junctions
   - Testing for waterproof integrity before tile installation

6. **Grouting, Sealing & Maintenance**
   - Cement-based and epoxy grouting techniques
   - Expansion joint placement and silicone caulking
   - Post-installation cleaning, haze removal, and tile protection
