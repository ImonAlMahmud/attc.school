Course Overview

The Tiler course at ATTC is designed to equip you with the skills and knowledge required to become a professional tiler in the construction and finishing industry. Tiling plays a crucial role in both the functionality and aesthetics of residential, commercial, and industrial spaces. This hands-on program focuses on preparing you to expertly lay tiles on walls, floors, and other surfaces using modern tools, materials, and techniques.

Course Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Understand different types of tiles and adhesives
  • Prepare surfaces for tile installation effectively
  • Measure, cut, and lay tiles with extreme precision
  • Apply tiles to various surfaces including floors, walls, and counters
  • Execute grouting and finishing work professionally
  • Maintain safety standards while working on-site

Course Curriculum

  • Role of a tiler in construction
  • Overview of tools, equipment, and safety gear
  • Types of tiles (ceramic, porcelain, mosaic, marble, granite)
  • Assessing and cleaning surfaces
  • Applying waterproofing and leveling compounds
  • Marking and layout planning
  • Cutting tiles accurately using hand and electric tools
  • Laying wall and floor tiles with proper spacing and alignment
  • Installing tiles on complex surfaces (corners, stairs, counters)
  • Mixing and applying grout
  • Cleaning, sealing, and finishing the tiled surface
  • Fixing tile defects and repairs
  • Decorative and patterned tiling
  • Tile installation in kitchens, bathrooms, and outdoor areas
  • Working with large format and specialty tiles
  • On-site safety protocols and PPE usage
  • Handling and storing materials properly
  • Identifying and managing potential hazards

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the Tiler course can pursue a variety of career paths, including:

  • Professional Tiler (Residential & Commercial)
  • Interior Finishing Technician
  • Construction Worker with tiling specialization
  • Self-Employed Tiling Contractor
