## Course Overview

The Formwork Carpentry course at ATTC provides thorough technical and practical training in constructing, erecting, and dismantling temporary mold systems for reinforced concrete structures. Formwork carpenters are crucial to ensuring building dimensions, surface finishes, and structural safety during concrete pouring and curing phases.

---

## Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Construct sturdy formwork systems for foundations, columns, beams, walls, and slabs
- Select appropriate shuttering materials (timber, plywood, steel, aluminum panels)
- Read and interpret structural engineering blueprints and formwork layouts
- Install propping, bracing, walers, tie rods, and scaffolding safely
- Ensure proper alignment, plumbness, and dimensional accuracy before concrete pours
- Safely strip and maintain formwork components for re-use

---

## Course Curriculum & Modules

1. **Introduction to Formwork Carpentry**
   - Role of shuttering and formwork in modern civil engineering
   - Properties of formwork materials (film-faced plywood, dimensional lumber, steel systems)
   - Carpentry hand and power tools: circular saws, levels, framing squares, drills

2. **Blueprint Reading & Formwork Design**
   - Interpreting architectural and structural details
   - Determining formwork dimensions, concrete load pressures, and support spacing
   - Material estimating and cut lists

3. **Column and Beam Formwork**
   - Fabricating column boxes and beam bottoms/sides
   - Installing column clamps, yokes, and kicker blocks
   - Leveling and plumbing column shutters

4. **Slab Formwork & Shoring Systems**
   - Erecting modular scaffolding, staging props, and stringer beams
   - Laying and sealing slab decking plywood
   - Drop-beam and cantilever shuttering techniques

5. **Wall Formwork & Tie Systems**
   - Assembling gang forms and panelized wall shutters
   - Installing heavy-duty tie rods, she-bolts, and cone spacers
   - Pouring rate considerations and concrete pressure management

6. **Striking, Maintenance & Safety**
   - Safe striking sequences and minimum curing times before formwork removal
   - Cleaning, oiling, and storing formwork materials for longevity
   - Working at heights and fall-protection safety

## Certification & Career Opportunities

Graduates receive the **ATTC Formwork Carpentry Certificate**, qualifying for:
- Formwork Carpenter / Shuttering Carpenter
- Scaffolding & Shoring Specialist
- Site Supervisor for Structural Teams
- International construction projects in Singapore, Middle East, and Europe
