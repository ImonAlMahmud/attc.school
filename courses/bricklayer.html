Course Overview

Master the timeless art and modern science of bricklaying. Build walls, arches, and intricate masonry structures with precision and craftsmanship — certified and job-ready from ATTC.

Course Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Mix mortar to correct proportions for structural strength
  • Lay bricks and blocks in stretcher, English, and Flemish bonds
  • Build plumb, level corners, piers, and cavity walls
  • Master joint finishing, pointing, and site cleanup

Course Curriculum

  • Masonry tools, equipment, and brick types
  • Fundamentals of mortar mixing
  • Stretcher, English, and Flemish bonds
  • Setting out first courses and maintaining levels
  • Building 9-inch and 4.5-inch walls
  • Constructing square corners, piers, and window/door openings
  • Cavity walls, insulation, and damp-proof courses (DPC)
  • Decorative brickwork and arch construction
  • Jointing and pointing styles
  • PPE, scaffolding safety, and site standards

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the Bricklayer course can pursue a variety of career paths, including:

  • Professional Bricklayer / Mason
  • Cavity Wall & Masonry Specialist
  • Masonry Contractor / Site Lead
  • International Building Technician
