Course Overview

The Plasterer course at ATTC is designed to provide you with the essential skills and practical knowledge required to become a professional plasterer. Plastering is a fundamental trade in the construction industry, essential for creating smooth, durable, and aesthetically pleasing surfaces on walls, ceilings, and other structures.

Course Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Understand the different types of plastering materials, tools, and equipment
  • Apply plaster effectively to various surfaces, ensuring smooth, even finishes
  • Learn key plastering techniques, such as applying base coats, finishing coats, and decorative plastering
  • Master the skills of working with drywall, skim coating, and repairing cracks and imperfections
  • Gain knowledge of plastering safety standards and work efficiently in a safe environment

Course Curriculum

  • Overview of plastering in the construction industry
  • Different types of plastering materials (cement, gypsum, lime)
  • Tools and equipment used in plastering
  • Preparing surfaces for plastering (walls, ceilings, and partitions)
  • Cleaning, priming, and ensuring surface stability
  • Applying base coats and finishing coats
  • Techniques for smooth finishes and texture applications
  • Skimming and floating techniques for drywall and plasterboard
  • Decorative plastering (cornices, moldings, and ornamental work)
  • Repairing and patching damaged plaster
  • Working with external plastering (weatherproof coatings, rendering)
  • Health and safety regulations for plasterers
  • Proper handling and disposal of materials
  • Personal protective equipment (PPE) and site safety

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the Plasterer course can pursue a variety of career paths, including:

  • Professional Plasterer (Residential, Commercial, Industrial)
  • Construction Worker with specialized plastering skills
  • Self-Employed Contractor or Freelancer
  • Site Supervisor or Team Leader in plastering projects
