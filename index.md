# Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC)

Empowering Skilled Professionals for Global Construction & Industry Excellence.

ATTC offers ISO-certified vocational courses on a modern 6-Bigha campus located in Bashundhara C/A, Dhaka. Get certified, master real-world skills, and build an international career.

## Key Highlights & Metrics

- **6-Bigha Modern Campus**: 2185/A, Block-I (Extension), Bashundhara C/A, Madani Avenue, Dhaka-1229, Bangladesh.
- **200+ Trainee Residential Facility**: Complete hostel accommodation with 24/7 power backup, Wi-Fi, and canteen.
- **100% Industry Certified**: Training programs recognized locally and globally.
- **5 Core Trade Programs**: Plasterer, Tiler, Formwork Carpentry, Bar Bending, and Bricklayer.

---

## Vocational Trade Courses

### 1. [Plasterer](https://www.attc.school/courses/plasterer.html)
Master professional plastering techniques using cement, gypsum, and lime on walls, ceilings, and architectural surfaces.
- **Duration**: Flexible / Intensive Trade Training
- **Certification**: ATTC Plasterer Certificate
- **Details**: [View Course Specification](https://www.attc.school/courses/plasterer.html)

### 2. [Tiler](https://www.attc.school/courses/tiler.html)
Comprehensive training in floor and wall tile installation, precision cutting, mosaic patterns, and grouting.
- **Duration**: Practical Hands-on Modules
- **Certification**: ATTC Tiler Certificate
- **Details**: [View Course Specification](https://www.attc.school/courses/tiler.html)

### 3. [Formwork Carpentry](https://www.attc.school/courses/formwork-carpentry.html)
Construct, erect, and dismantle timber and steel formwork systems for reinforced concrete slabs, columns, and beams.
- **Duration**: Trade Assessment & Practical Training
- **Certification**: ATTC Formwork Carpentry Certificate
- **Details**: [View Course Specification](https://www.attc.school/courses/formwork-carpentry.html)

### 4. [Bar Bending](https://www.attc.school/courses/bar-bending.html)
Learn rebar cutting, manual and machine bending, tying schedules, and reinforcement cage assembly.
- **Duration**: Comprehensive Practical Workshop
- **Certification**: ATTC Bar Bending Certificate
- **Details**: [View Course Specification](https://www.attc.school/courses/bar-bending.html)

### 5. [Bricklayer](https://www.attc.school/courses/bricklayer.html)
Master bricklaying, masonry bonds, mortar mixing, level alignment, and structural wall construction.
- **Duration**: Hands-on Masonry Training
- **Certification**: ATTC Bricklayer Certificate
- **Details**: [View Course Specification](https://www.attc.school/courses/bricklayer.html)
