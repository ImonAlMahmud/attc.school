# About Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC)

Unlocking human potential through specialized hands-on technical training, aligned with global construction standards.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple yet profound – to bridge the gap between education and industry needs. We equip students with practical skills, hands-on experience, and a comprehensive understanding of their chosen disciplines. Through our courses, we strive to empower individuals to not only secure employment but to excel in their careers and contribute meaningfully to their industries.

- **Nurturing Real Skills**: Going beyond traditional classroom learning with hands-on practice in specialized workshops.
- **Future-Ready Programs**: State-of-the-art facilities and expert instructors ensuring trainees are globally competitive.

---

## Campus & Facilities

ATTC operates on a **6-Bigha modern campus** situated in Bashundhara C/A, Madani Avenue, Dhaka:
- **Residential Hostel**: Accommodation for 200+ students with 24/7 security and warden care.
- **Canteen**: Clean dining hall serving 100+ trainees per batch.
- **Modern Testing Labs**: Trade-specific testing bays equipped with international standard machinery.
- **High-Speed Connectivity**: Campus-wide high-speed Wi-Fi network.
- **Continuous Power Backup**: 24/7 industrial diesel generator backup.
