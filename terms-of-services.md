# Terms of Service — Advance Training & Testing Center (ATTC)

**Effective Date**: July 5, 2025  
**Domain**: https://www.attc.school

By enrolling in any vocational training program, accessing the campus facilities, or using this website, you agree to comply with the terms and conditions outlined below.

---

## 1. Vocational Training Services

ATTC provides practical, ISO-certified vocational courses in:
- **Plastering**
- **Tiling**
- **Formwork Carpentry**
- **Bar Bending**
- **Bricklaying**

These courses combine workshop demonstrations, safety instruction, tool mastery, and hands-on site simulations.

---

## 2. Enrollment & Eligibility Requirements

- **Age Requirement**: All trainees must be at least 18 years of age.
- **Documentation**: Submitting verified identity documents (NID/Passport/Birth Certificate) is mandatory prior to class start.
- **Physical Fitness**: Candidates must be medically fit to participate in manual trade training.

---

## 3. Tuition, Payments & Refund Policy

- Course fees must be paid in full at the time of official admission or per agreed installment schedule.
- **Refund Policy**: Fees paid are non-refundable once classes have commenced, except where mandated by law or formal center cancellation.

---

## 4. Campus Accommodation & Code of Conduct

Students utilizing the 200-seat on-campus dormitory agree to:
- Follow hostel curfews and maintain discipline and clean habits.
- Adhere strictly to site safety guidelines and wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) in workshops.
- Treat instructors, staff, and fellow trainees with respect.

---

## 5. Certification Conditions

- ATTC certificates are awarded upon satisfactory attendance (minimum 85%) and successful completion of all practical trade assessments.
